# Aegis Secure Business Specification & Commercialization Strategy

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Value Proposition

**Aegis Secure** solves the growing crisis of mobile spyware, smishing attacks, public Wi-Fi interception, and device theft for both privacy-conscious individuals and enterprise workforces. By unifying threat scanning, privacy killswitches, zero-log VPN, zero-knowledge vaulting, and AI scam defense in a single lightweight suite, Aegis delivers unparalleled protection without degrading device battery life or system performance.

---

## 2. Market Opportunity & Total Addressable Market (TAM)

- **Global Mobile Security Market**: Projected to exceed **$18.4 Billion by 2028**, growing at a **19.8% CAGR**.
- **Target Segments**:
  1. **High-Risk Individuals**: Journalists, executives, legal professionals, crypto asset holders, and political figures.
  2. **Enterprise Remote Fleets**: SMBs and Fortune 500 corporations seeking BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) mobile endpoint security.
  3. **Privacy-Conscious Consumers**: Everyday smartphone users seeking protection against identity theft and scam SMS messages.
